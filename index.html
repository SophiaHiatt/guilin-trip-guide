桂林 9 月底 – 10 月初气候背景Guilin Climate: Late Sep – Early Oct

气候
Climate
全年温和，气温大致 8–28°C；最佳游玩期 4–10 月。此时段日间约 26–32°C、夜间约 20–24°C，湿度偏高（70–85%），体感闷热。
Mild year-round, roughly 8–28°C; best season Apr–Oct. In this window expect about 26–32°C by day, 20–24°C at night, with high humidity (70–85%) and a muggy feel.
降雨
Rain
桂林多雨，秋季仍常有 短时阵雨或雷阵雨，来得快去得也快。建议随身带 折叠伞 / 轻便雨衣，室外项目遇雨会临时调整。
Guilin is rainy; autumn still brings short showers or thunderstorms, quick to arrive and quick to pass. Carry a folding umbrella / light rain jacket; outdoor activities are rescheduled when it rains.
日照 / 蚊虫
Sun / Mosquitoes
紫外线较强，水面与草地反光明显；傍晚蚊虫较多（村内有沙滩、湖水、绿地）。
UV is strong with glare off water and grass; mosquitoes are active around dusk (beach, lake and lawns on site).

着装建议What to Pack & Wear

按当日实时气温自动提示Live tip based on temperature 加载中… Loading…
  • 白天：速干短袖 T 恤 / 透气长裤；防晒帽、墨镜、防晒霜（SPF50+）必备。
  • Daytime: quick-dry T-shirts and breathable trousers; sun hat, sunglasses and SPF50+ sunscreen are essential.
  • 运动项目：必须穿 运动衣 + 运动鞋（园区规定，未着运动服/鞋无法参加）。攀岩需 长裤。
  • Sports: Sportswear and sports shoes are compulsory (you will be turned away otherwise). Climbing requires long trousers.
  • 空中飞人：不要穿裙子，不要穿带纽扣、拉链、皮带的衣服；参加前不要涂防晒霜（会打滑）。
  • Flying trapeze: no skirts, no buttons, zips or belts; do not apply sunscreen before the activity (it makes grips slippery).
  • 泳池 / 戏水：泳衣泳帽、防水袋、快干浴巾；幼儿建议带 游泳尿布 和臂圈（泳池可免费借用臂圈与浴巾）。
  • Pools / water play: swimwear, swim cap, dry bag and a quick-dry towel; for toddlers bring swim nappies and armbands (armbands and towels are free at the pool).
  • 夜间：每晚有 Dress Code 主题着装（常见为蓝、白、红、黑、粉、牛仔、亚洲风情等），自愿参与，建议多带几件不同颜色的衣服与配饰。剧场冷气较足，可备薄外套。
  • Evening: each night has a dress-code theme (blue, white, red, black, pink, denim, Asian elegance, etc.). Participation is optional but fun — pack a few coloured outfits and accessories. The theatre is air-conditioned, so bring a light layer.
  • 其他：驱蚊液、常备药（儿童退烧/腹泻药）、保温水杯（园区驿站可补水）、小推车（5 岁以下强烈建议带）。
  • Also bring: mosquito repellent, basic medicines (fever/diarrhoea for kids), a water bottle (refill stations at the Lounge), and a light stroller (strongly advised under age 5).
  • 雨天备用：室内恒温泳池、室内攀岩、室内空中飞人、VR、篮球/羽毛球/乒乓球、健身房均正常开放，雨天不会无聊。
  • Rainy-day backup: the heated indoor pool, indoor climbing, indoor trapeze, VR, basketball/badminton/table tennis and the gym all stay open — rain does not mean boredom.

Dining: Hours · Signature Dishes · What to Note

一价全包含一日三餐 + 下午茶 + 夜宵 + 酒吧畅饮；凭手环自由进出

All-inclusive covers three meals, afternoon tea, late-night snacks and free-flow bars; just show your wristband

最容易被忽略、也最需要提前做的一件事 The one thing most guests miss 蒙古餐厅与莲餐厅不每天开放，且不能提前多天预订——须在开放当日早上 07:00 起到 Atelier 主餐厅门口现场登记（部分资料为 08:30 起）。每人限订 4 位（含儿童），额满即止。建议当天早餐前先去排队。
两个餐厅通常轮流开放、不同时开，且以每周活动安排为准——入住当天先到前台或主餐厅问清楚本周哪几天开哪个。 The Mongolian BBQ and Lotus restaurants are not open daily and cannot be booked days in advance — you must sign up on the day itself from 07:00 at the entrance of The Atelier main restaurant (some sources say 08:30). Capacity is 4 seats per person, children included, on a first-come basis. Go before breakfast.
They usually alternate rather than open on the same day, following the weekly programme — ask Reception or The Atelier on arrival day which nights each one runs.

家长必读Parents' Checklist

  • 17:00 之后托管另收费：200 元 / 小时 / 人。请准时接回。
  • Care after 17:00 is charged at CNY 200 per hour per child. Please collect on time.
  • 每天要带：运动鞋、袜子、泳衣（几乎每天都有游泳环节）、水杯、防晒帽/防晒衣。泳衣和运动鞋最容易忘。
  • Bring daily: sports shoes, socks, swimwear (swimming features almost every day), water bottle, sun hat / UV shirt. Swimwear and trainers are the two most-forgotten items.
  • 建议不带私人玩具与电子产品，G.O 不负责保管；园区提供毛巾与饮用水。
  • Please leave at home personal toys and electronics; G.O.s cannot look after them. Towels and drinking water are provided.
  • 分流规则：4 岁以下不能进迷你俱乐部，需付费报「小小俱乐部」（2–3 岁）。11 岁以上转青少年俱乐部。
  • Age rules: under 4s cannot join Mini Club and need the paid Petit Club (2–3). Ages 11+ move to the Teens Club.
  • 若孩子当天未托管，安全与看管由家长负责。
  • On days your child is not in the club, supervision is the parents' responsibility.
  • 换洗衣物多备一套：戏水、手工、户外探索都可能弄湿或弄脏。
  • Pack a spare set of clothes: water play, crafts and outdoor exploration all get messy.

带 3 岁半孩子的实操建议Practical Tips

  • 一天安排两块就够：上午 09:00–11:00 户外（喂鸭子 / footgolf / 漫步），午睡后 15:00–17:00 戏水或 Amazing Family，晚上 20:15 家庭秀。3 岁半的耐力不允许排满。
  • Two blocks a day is plenty: 09:00–11:00 outdoors (ducks, footgolf, strolls), 15:00–17:00 after the nap for water play or Amazing Family, then the 20:15 family show. A 3½-year-old cannot manage a full schedule.
  • 正午 12:00–15:00 一定躲进室内（午餐 + 午睡），桂林 9–10 月紫外线与湿度对幼儿负担较重。
  • Hide indoors 12:00–15:00 (lunch plus nap); Guilin's late-September UV and humidity are hard on toddlers.
  • 必备清单：轻便推车、游泳尿布、臂圈、速干衣 3 套、宽檐帽、驱蚊液、常备药、保温杯、小零食。
  • Pack list: light stroller, swim nappies, armbands, three sets of quick-dry clothes, wide-brim hat, repellent, basic medicines, a flask and snacks.
  • 安全边界：水边、雕塑石径、电瓶车上下车、泳池派对人群是四个高风险场景，视线不离人。
  • Safety watch-outs: water's edge, sculpture-park stone paths, getting on and off buggies, and crowded pool parties — keep them in sight at all times.
  • 想要大人独处时间：只能走付费小小俱乐部（2–3 岁），需带儿童健康手册；建议先试半天（235 元），观察孩子适应再做全天。
  • Want adult-only time: the only route is the paid Petit Club (2–3), and you must bring the child health booklet. Try a half day first (CNY 235) before committing to a full day.

如果你更想让长辈看孩子If Grandparents Are Taking the Toddler

  • 推荐组合：上午陪长辈一起参加长青乐的「晨间健走 / 文化艺术游览」——推车在园区可行，孩子也能看到雕塑与湖水；长者活动节奏慢，很适合带娃同行。
  • Good pairing: join the Golden Moments morning walk or art tour with grandparents — the park is stroller-friendly, toddlers enjoy the sculptures and lake, and senior sessions are slow enough for a little one.
  • 午后：长者可在主吧台参与茶道 / 电影 / 麻将（室内有空调），孩子由另一位家长带去戏水，分工明确。
  • Afternoon: grandparents take tea ceremony, movie or mahjong at the main bar (air-conditioned) while the other parent takes the toddler to the water — a clean division of labour.
  • 注意：长青乐的「油茶制作」等部分项目涉及热水、工具，不建议幼儿在场；建议选茶道、电影、文化艺术游览、喂鸭子这类低风险项目。
  • Note: some Golden Moments sessions (oil-tea making) involve hot water and tools, so not ideal with a toddler. Prefer tea ceremony, movies, art tours and duck feeding.
  • 集合与交通：长者活动多在驿站、瑜伽室、主吧台等室内点集合，园区电瓶车 07:00–22:30 招手即停，带着推车出行不必担心距离。
  • Meet points: senior sessions mostly gather indoors at the Lounge, yoga room or main bar; buggies run 07:00–22:30, so distance is never an issue with a stroller.
